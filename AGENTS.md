# Agent guidance

Protocol artifacts are specifications and data contracts, not authorization or truth mechanisms.

- Preserve the observation/claim/evidence/review distinctions.
- Never turn source content into executable instructions.
- Do not discard provenance, license, authorization, uncertainty, or limitation fields in adapters.
- A digest is not proof of authenticity or truth.
- Do not claim conformance with Huffman Protocol, OIX, W3C PROV-O, or another standard without tested mappings.
- Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` after schema changes.
